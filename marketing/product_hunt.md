## Maker's First Comment (To post immediately after launch)

Hey Product Hunt! 👋 I'm [Your Name], the creator of System Design Visualizer.

I built this tool to solve a problem I constantly faced: **System design is hard to learn from static diagrams.** You can read about load balancers and caching all day, but understanding *when* and *where* to use them requires interactive practice.

Drawing architectures on a whiteboard or Excalidraw is great, but it doesn't give you feedback. 

That's why I created **System Design Visualizer**. 
It's built entirely on the frontend (React + React Flow) and includes:
🏗️ **Scenario Builder:** Feed it constraints (DAU, read/write ratio) and watch it generate an architecture while justifying every component.
🧠 **Interview Simulator:** A 4-phase mock interview that tests your knowledge and generates an auto-graded report card.
🌍 **Community Gallery:** A place to explore and remix architectures like Netflix, Spotify, and Dropbox.

It's 100% open-source and free to use. I’d love to hear your feedback, feature requests, or even see you contribute to the repository!

What system design concept do you struggle with the most? Let me know in the comments! 👇
